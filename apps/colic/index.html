Help Your Baby Sleep Better

Colic Baby uses proven white noise sounds to soothe your baby and help the whole family get better rest. Simple, effective, and privacy-focused.

Features

🔊

12 White Noise Sounds

From vacuum cleaner to rain, heartbeat to fan - carefully selected sounds proven to calm babies.

⏱️

Smart Timer

Set a sleep timer from 15 to 120 minutes with gentle fade-out. Saves battery, helps baby sleep naturally.

📱

Background Play

Continue using your phone while sounds play. Works with screen off to save battery.

🔕

Call Detection

Automatically pauses when you receive a phone call and resumes after.

🔒

Privacy First

No data collection, no ads, no tracking. Your privacy is our priority.

🌍

Multi-Language

Available in 12 languages including English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, and more.
