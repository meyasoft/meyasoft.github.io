About this policy

Meyasoft Yazılım provides Colic Baby for adult parents and caregivers. Sound playback works offline. The ad-supported Android version uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

1 Information Collected

Colic Baby does not require an account or ask for a child's name, contact information, or precise location. On the ad-supported Android version, Google Mobile Ads may collect and share your IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID when available. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

2 Permissions and Storage

  • Foreground Service and Wake Lock: Allow sounds to continue playing while the screen is off.
  • Notifications: Show playback controls.
  • Network access and advertising identifiers: Support ads and ad privacy choices in the ad-supported Android version.

Audio files are stored on the device. Listening does not require a network connection.

3 Advertising and Privacy Choices

Google AdMob provides banner ads. UMP displays privacy choices where applicable. In the Android app, tap the logo to open About, then select Ad privacy options when it is available. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4 Children’s Privacy

Colic Baby is designed for adult parents and caregivers to operate. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us.

5 Security and Retention

Google reports that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts data in transit. Google describes retention for its services at How Google retains data it collects. If you email us, we use your message and email address to respond and retain the correspondence as needed to handle the request.

6 Your Rights and Policy Changes

You can contact us with privacy questions or requests. For information processed by Google, review Google's privacy policy and the ad privacy options available in the app. We will post changes to this policy here and update the date above.
