Privacy First for Your Little One

Sleepy Baby does not collect, record, track, or share any personal information.

1. Information Collection and Use

Sleepy Baby is built specifically to operate offline and respect family privacy. We do not require account creation, logins, or personal details.

Timer durations and sound preferences are stored locally on your device only.

2. Audio and Device Permissions

The app only requests standard audio output capabilities to play soothing sounds and background audio to ensure sleep sounds continue when your screen turns off.

3. Children's Privacy

Sleepy Baby complies with strict children's privacy standards (including COPPA). The app does not collect personal data from children or any other users.
