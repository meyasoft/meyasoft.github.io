Your Privacy Matters

Royal Chicken Clash is designed for casual fun and respects your personal privacy.

1. Information Collection & Usage

Royal Chicken Clash does not require personal registration or account creation to play. We do not collect or store personal identifying information such as names, phone numbers, or physical addresses.

Any game progress or high scores are saved locally on your device or via standard Apple Game Center APIs if enabled by the user.

2. Advertising & Analytics

The game may display contextual advertisements to support ongoing development. Advertising networks may use randomized, non-personal device identifiers to deliver relevant ads in accordance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) guidelines.

3. Children's Privacy

Royal Chicken Clash does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.
